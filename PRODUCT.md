# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
- Primary: owners and decision-makers at local businesses in the Gunnison Valley who need a trustworthy partner to improve or create a digital product.
- Secondary: founders and teams beyond the region seeking an experienced partner for web, mobile, SaaS, AI, and automation work.
- Visitors are often not deeply technical. They need to understand relevance, credibility, and working fit quickly without decoding implementation jargon.

## Product Purpose
The Crest Code Creative portfolio turns completed work into clear evidence that Crest Code can understand a business problem, shape the right product, and deliver a dependable result. It should help qualified visitors decide that a conversation is worth starting.

## Positioning
Crest Code is a hands-on product partner with local roots and broad technical range. It combines the care and accessibility of a small studio with the product judgment and engineering depth required for sophisticated digital products.

## Operating Context
- The portfolio is a public marketing and sales surface viewed on mobile and desktop.
- Many visitors will arrive from referrals, local search, or service pages and will scan before reading deeply.
- The index must establish range and credibility quickly; individual case studies must reward deeper evaluation without becoming technical documentation.
- A successful visit builds enough confidence for the visitor to begin a qualified project conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Projects are sourced from MDX frontmatter and long-form MDX content in `content/projects`.
- The current portfolio includes local wellness work alongside mobile, SaaS, AI, automation, and full-stack product engagements.
- Existing project imagery and truthful project claims must remain the source of evidence; the design must not invent clients, metrics, testimonials, or outcomes.
- Routes, metadata, static generation, external project links, responsive behavior, and accessible navigation must remain functional.
- The portfolio should work as a coherent subsystem within the existing Crest Code site without requiring a full-site redesign.

## Brand Commitments
- Approachable expertise: explain sophisticated work in plain language.
- Partnership: show understanding, judgment, and collaboration—not just implementation output.
- Credibility: favor specific evidence and legible case-study structure over inflated claims.
- Local grounding: retain the Gunnison Valley connection without implying the studio only serves local clients.
- Thoughtful craft: the experience should feel deliberate, calm, contemporary, and human.

## Evidence on Hand
- Five published case studies: ThirdTone, Yoga Flow, Link Loom, TeamCrest, and Earthy Soul Living.
- Project imagery, technology lists, challenge/solution/impact summaries, and detailed MDX narratives.
- One client testimonial from Earthy Soul Living with a named client and portrait.
- Existing public links for projects where available.
- Founder story, local history, mission, values, and Gunnison Valley location on the About page.
- Demonstrated capabilities across web, mobile, SaaS, AI, subscriptions, content operations, cloud infrastructure, and business workflows.

## Product Principles
1. Lead with recognizable business problems and outcomes; support them with technical depth.
2. Make credibility scannable before asking for sustained attention.
3. Treat each case study as a narrative: context, challenge, intervention, evidence, and result.
4. Let the diversity of the work demonstrate range while maintaining one clear partner story.
5. Give every page a natural next step toward another relevant project or a qualified conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard navigation, semantic structure, and reduced-motion preferences.
- Do not rely on hover, color, or imagery alone to communicate project meaning or actions.
- Keep body copy readable, controls comfortably sized, and layouts robust at narrow viewport widths.
- Use direct, jargon-light language that welcomes non-technical business owners while preserving optional technical detail.
