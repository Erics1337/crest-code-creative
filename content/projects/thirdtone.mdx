## Project Overview

ThirdTone is a precision sound-medicine platform that helps listeners discover and follow audio protocols aligned with their goals. Crest Code worked across the entire product ecosystem: the consumer mobile experience, a content-rich website, an operations portal, the shared backend, and the media infrastructure connecting them.

## The Experience

### Personal Listening Protocols

- Short, goal-oriented onboarding
- AI-assisted content scoring and daily recommendations
- Personalized narrative cards that explain each day's practice
- Listening progress, favorites, and content discovery

### Mobile Product

- Native-feeling iOS and Android experience built with Expo
- Rich audio playback for tones, journeys, and collections
- Subscription access and entitlement management
- Push notifications, analytics, and production error monitoring

### Content and Editorial Operations

- Next.js portal for audio, imagery, tags, and categories
- AI-assisted metadata workflows for a growing catalog
- Astro website with journal content and editor-controlled homepage sections

## Technical Implementation

The product is organized as a Bun monorepo with a Convex backend and separate mobile, admin, and web applications. Clerk handles authentication, RevenueCat manages subscription entitlements, Cloudflare R2 stores media, and a dedicated FFmpeg worker creates streaming formats and waveform data.

## Result

The platform brings acquisition, personalization, playback, progress, billing, and editorial control into one coherent system. Its architecture supports a growing media catalog without compromising the quiet, intentional character of the listener experience.
