## Project Overview

Yoga Flow is a mobile practice companion for creating yoga around the time, style, focus, pace, and comfort needs of the person practicing. Crest Code designed and engineered the experience from the visual system and native interaction patterns through cloud sync, AI generation, payments, and release infrastructure.

## Key Features

### Flexible Sequence Creation

- Start with a curated practice or design a new sequence with AI
- Tune duration, style, focus, pace, intensity, and comfort considerations
- Add a personal intention, limitation, or mood in natural language

### Hands-On Flow Editor

- Add, reorder, group, and remove poses
- Adjust rounds, sides, breath counts, and section timing
- Detect awkward transitions and suggest bridge poses or nearby edits

### Guided Practice

- Clear visual pose cues and progress
- Next-pose previews and adjustable pacing
- Configurable audio coaching, from pose names to full guidance
- Haptic feedback and a focused, hands-free presentation

### Production Product Foundation

- Supabase authentication and cloud-synced sequence library
- RevenueCat subscriptions and AI generation entitlements
- English, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese localization
- Sentry monitoring and automated unit coverage for core flows

## Result

Yoga Flow is a complete consumer product rather than a simple timer or static pose library. The final system supports the full journey from an idea for today's practice to a guided, saved sequence that can be revisited on another device.
