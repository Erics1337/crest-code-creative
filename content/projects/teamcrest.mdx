## Project Overview

TeamCrest is a multi-tenant workforce operations platform for businesses managing hourly employees. It began with the specialized needs of aquatic and recreation facilities—where coverage depends on roles and certifications and recurring safety records matter—and has grown into a flexible foundation for teams across industries.

## Key Features

### AI-Powered Scheduling
- Build schedules around staff availability, roles, certifications, and recurring shift patterns
- Surface coverage conflicts and open shifts before a schedule is published

### Team, Availability, and Time
- Manage employees, departments, permissions, qualifications, and availability
- Track hours, review timesheets, and organize approvals around configurable pay periods

### Compliance Workflows
- Track recurring safety and operational responsibilities
- Give managers a clear readiness view for required records and staff setup

### Dynamic AI Chatbot Assistant
- Virtual manager grounded in business-specific documents and a private knowledge base
- Answers employee questions, supports training, and assists daily tasks

### Issue Tracking System
- Log, assign, and manage workplace issues for accountability and smoother operations

### Comprehensive Management Tools
- Targeted email and SMS communication, live notifications, and shift updates
- Checklists, digital forms, reporting, open shifts, and real-time availability
- Multi-workspace roles, permissions, and subscription billing

## Technical Implementation

- **Next.js + TypeScript** for a fast, maintainable application framework
- **PostgreSQL and Supabase** for multi-tenant data, authentication, real-time updates, and row-level security
- **AI integrations** for scheduling assistance and the document-grounded virtual manager
- **Stripe** for subscription plans and account billing
- **Tailwind CSS** for a streamlined UI system

## Results and Impact

- One operational view across scheduling, availability, hours, compliance, and communication
- Fewer manual handoffs when schedules or staffing needs change
- Faster onboarding and day-to-day support through the virtual manager

## Product Direction

The platform is continuing to deepen schedule automation, compliance readiness, and employee self-service while preserving secure tenant boundaries and the flexibility required by different hourly-work environments.
